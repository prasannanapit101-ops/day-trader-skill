# NEPSE Trading Discipline and Ironclad Execution Rules

To be followed strictly for all Nepal Stock Exchange (NEPSE) trade planning and signals.

## 1. The Anti-Trap Resistance Rule (The HPPL Lesson)
- NEVER enter a trade within 3% of a major resistance level, 52-week high, or swing high, regardless of how bullish the earnings or news are.
- Fundamental catalysts (Q1 earnings, dividends, bonus shares) do NOT justify buying into a technical ceiling.
- Resistance breakout rule: Require a confirmed candle CLOSE above the resistance level on volume exceeding 1.5x average. Anticipation without confirmation is strictly forbidden.
- Minimum Reward-to-Risk ratio to immediate resistance must be at least 1.5:1. If resistance sits 1.5% away and stop is 4% away, the trade is BLOCKED.

## 2. NEPSE Market Constraints
- Settlement: T+2 (CDSC / MeroShare EDIS). No same-day round trips on fresh purchases.
- Short Selling: Prohibited in NEPSE cash market. All trades are long-only.
- Circuit Bands: Daily limit is +/- 10% per scrip. Orders within 1.5% of upper circuit are BLOCKED.
- Market Halts: 4% (20 min), 5% (40 min), 6% (day close). No orders during market halts.
- Trading Schedule: Sunday through Thursday, 11:00 AM to 3:00 PM NPT.
- Entry Window: 11:15 AM to 2:00 PM NPT. Never in pre-open (10:30-10:45 AM) or first 15 mins.

## 3. Floor Sheet Footprint (Pelosi Tracker Equivalent)
- Cross-check top accumulating vs dumping brokers (e.g., Broker 58, 45, 38, 17, 4).
- If top sellers are dumping massive blocks (>1 lakh shares), cancel buy proposals immediately.

## 4. Post-Trade Compliance
- EDIS transfer on MeroShare must be completed before 6:00 PM on the trade day to avoid the mandatory 20% close-out penalty.
